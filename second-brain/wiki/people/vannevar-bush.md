## Overview

Vannevar Bush (1890–1974) was an American engineer and science administrator who directed the U.S. Office of Scientific Research and Development during WWII. In the context of this wiki, he is significant as the originator of the **Memex** concept (1945) — a direct intellectual precursor to the LLM Wiki Pattern.

## Key Facts

- Published "As We May Think" in The Atlantic, July 1945 — the foundational essay describing the Memex.
- The Memex was a hypothetical device for storing and retrieving a person's books, records, and communications, with the ability to create "associative trails" between documents.
- Bush identified the core problem: human knowledge was growing faster than our ability to organize and access it. His proposed solution was a personal, curated knowledge store with rich cross-references.
- The problem Bush could not solve: **who maintains the cross-references and keeps the knowledge current**. The LLM Wiki Pattern answers this: the LLM does the maintenance.
- The Memex was never built; its influence shaped the development of hypertext and the web (Ted Nelson cited Bush directly).
