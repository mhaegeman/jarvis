## Overview

Jesse Vincent is the creator and primary maintainer of [Superpowers](../entities/superpowers.md), a skills-based workflow plugin for AI coding agents. He runs [Prime Radiant](../entities/prime-radiant.md), the company behind Superpowers. He blogs at blog.fsck.com where he published the original Superpowers writeup ("Superpowers for Claude Code", 2025-10-09).

## Key Facts

- Creator of Superpowers (`obra/superpowers` on GitHub)
- Founder / operator of Prime Radiant
- Active open-source maintainer; known for tight quality control (94% PR rejection rate on Superpowers)
- Explicit philosophy: treat skills as code, not prose; require eval evidence for behavioral changes
