## Overview

Boris Cherny is the creator of [Claude Code](../entities/claude-code.md) at Anthropic. Engineering background. Publicly shares his Claude Code workflow and philosophy through interviews, tweets, and conference appearances. Known for a disciplined, minimal approach: plan before building, keep CLAUDE.md lean, close verification loops, and assume models will keep improving faster than any scaffold you build.

## Key Facts

- **Role**: Creator of Claude Code at Anthropic
- **Philosophy**: "Never bet against the model" — references Rich Sutton's Bitter Lesson as a core framing principle (has a framed copy on the wall)
- **Workflow**: Plan Mode first (~80% of sessions), minimal CLAUDE.md (~couple thousand tokens), verification loops, parallel sessions with partitioned tasks, slash commands for inner loops
- **On CLAUDE.md**: Recommends deleting and starting fresh when it gets bloated — "do the minimal possible thing to get the model on track"
- **On verification**: Giving Claude a feedback loop to check its own work 2–3x's the quality of results
