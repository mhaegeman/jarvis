## Overview

Daniel Kokotajlo is the lead author of [AI 2027](../sources/ai-2027.md) and a former OpenAI safety researcher. Named to TIME100. His prior AI forecasting work has a notable track record of accuracy.

## Key Facts

- Former OpenAI researcher; departed (covered in a 2024 NYT piece on OpenAI culture and whistleblowers).
- **Forecasting track record:** His August 2021 scenario "What 2026 Looks Like" predicted chain-of-thought reasoning, inference scaling, chip export controls, and $100M training runs — all more than a year before ChatGPT's release. The prediction was evaluated and held up well.
- Lead author of AI 2027 alongside Eli Lifland, Thomas Larsen, Romeo Dean; prose by Scott Alexander.
- As of July 2025, his all-things-considered median for Superhuman Coder arrival is approximately 2030 (updated from earlier ~2027 estimate, after adjusting for outside-of-model factors).
