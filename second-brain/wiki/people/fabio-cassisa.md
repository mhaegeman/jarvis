## Overview

Fabio Cassisa is a design-to-technology hybrid based in the Malmö/Copenhagen area. He evolved from industrial design into AI engineering and creative development, currently working in ad tech at Adnami as an AI engineer/architect building complex agents. He is a friend of [Maxime Haegeman](maxime-haegeman.md) and a potential collaborator on the GuardRail project — the EU AI Act compliance platform.
