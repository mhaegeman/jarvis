## Summary

Fabio Cassisa is a design-to-technology hybrid — an Italian-born, Scandinavia-based professional who evolved from industrial design into AI engineering and creative development. He currently works in ad tech at Adnami, where he builds high-impact ad formats and internal tooling, but describes his role more broadly as "AI engineer/architect for complex agents + creative developer" — a self-described "unicorn full-stack + design position."

His career traces a deliberate arc from design (industrial, graphic, interaction) through front-end development and blockchain (Solidity, ERC-721 tokens for Art Value) to his current focus on AI agent architecture. He co-founded 4Foodie, a food network startup where he served as developer/designer and shareholder for nearly 3 years. His academic work includes a course on AI Ethics, Regulation, and Politics at Malmö University (grade: B) and a course on Research Frontiers in Digitalization at the University of Gothenburg.

Fabio is a friend of [Maxime Haegeman](../people/maxime-haegeman.md) and a potential collaborator on the [GuardRail](../../../ideas.md) project — the EU AI Act compliance platform. His combination of AI agent architecture experience, full-stack creative development skills, and AI ethics coursework makes him a strong addition to the team.
