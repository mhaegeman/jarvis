[Maxime Haegeman](../people/maxime-haegeman.md) is a Senior Data / ML Engineer based in Copenhagen, Denmark, with 5 years of experience building data pipelines, ML models, and analytics systems. His positioning: bridging the gap between models and production — "From messy data to reliable predictions, at scale." He is currently at Massive Entertainment (Ubisoft Studio) in Malmö, Sweden, leading ML and analytics engineering. He maintains a personal portfolio at maximehaegeman.com and a GitHub at [mhaegeman](https://github.com/mhaegeman).

He has a broad, production-focused technical profile: strong in Python/PySpark/SQL, experienced with the modern data stack (Databricks, Airflow, Snowflake, BigQuery, Delta Lake), and capable across the ML stack (TensorFlow, PyTorch, HuggingFace, SpaCy, MLflow, XGBoost). His work spans ETL pipelines, propensity models, NLP products (sentiment analysis, text generation, speech-to-text, transformer fine-tuning), and most recently live game analytics infrastructure.
