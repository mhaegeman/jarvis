## Overview

Airbnb is the US travel-marketplace company whose engineering org originated and open-sourced Chronon (formerly Zipline), the feature platform that powers all of Airbnb's major ML applications. Airbnb holds 8 of the 13 PMC seats on Chronon and shares maintenance with Stripe.

## Key Facts

- Originated Chronon internally as Zipline, later open-sourced and renamed.
- Uses Chronon to power "all major ML applications within Airbnb" (per `docs/getting_started/Introduction.md`).
- Holds 8 of 13 PMC seats on the Chronon project; co-governs with Stripe.
- Apache 2.0 licensing stance; governance modeled on Apache-style projects (CHIP process, lazy consensus, dev@ mailing list).
