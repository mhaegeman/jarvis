## Overview

nexos.ai is an all-in-one enterprise AI platform ($350M valuation) founded by the NordVPN/Nord Security team. It provides unified multi-LLM access with built-in guardrails, governance, observability, and no-code AI agents. It is referenced in the [GuardRail project](../../../ideas.md) as the primary named competitor, but the competitive overlap is low: nexos.ai helps orgs *use AI safely* (workspace + gateway + input/output filtering), while GuardRail helps orgs *comply with the EU AI Act* (risk classification + documentation + conformity assessment).

## Key Facts

- **Valuation:** $350M (reported funding; NordVPN founders).
- **Product pillars:** AI Workspace, AI Gateway, AI Guardrails, AI Governance, AI Agents.
- **"Guardrails" = input/output filtering** (PII detection, sensitive data blocking, policy enforcement). Not AI Act compliance.
- **"Governance" = AI usage monitoring** (token spend, model access, audit trails, budget controls). Not regulatory compliance.
- **Certifications:** GDPR, SOC 2 Type 1, ISO 27001. Hosted in Europe.
- **Target:** enterprise teams across all departments. No mid-market self-serve focus.
- **Integrations:** Slack, SharePoint, Google Drive, Confluence, ActiveFence, Pangea.
- **Press:** Bloomberg, TechCrunch, Forbes, TechRadar.
- **Mobile apps:** iOS and Android.
- **Revenue model:** SaaS subscription (freemium, 7-day trial). No public pricing.
