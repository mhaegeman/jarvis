## Overview

Massive Entertainment is a Ubisoft Studio based in Malmö, Sweden, known for developing open-world games (The Division series, Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora, Star Wars Outlaws). It is the current employer of [Maxime Haegeman](../people/maxime-haegeman.md), who joined as a Data Engineer in March 2024.

## Key Facts

- **Parent company**: Ubisoft
- **Location**: Malmö, Sweden
- **Website**: [massive.se](https://www.massive.se/)
- **Known titles**: The Division, The Division 2, Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora, Star Wars Outlaws
- **Maxime's role**: Data Engineer — ML and analytics pipelines, Azure Databricks migration, live game analytics with Grafana, core shared Python library (Mar 2024–Present)
- **Harsh's role**: UX Researcher & Product Designer — worked on Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora and Star Wars Outlaws
