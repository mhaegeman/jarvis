## Overview

Meta AI Research, operating under the FAIR (Fundamental AI Research) banner, is Meta's core AI research division. It produces foundational open-source AI models and datasets, with particular strength in computer vision and multi-modal AI. FAIR operates under a philosophy of publishing research and releasing model weights openly, in contrast to closed-model labs.

## Key Facts

- Created SAM (Segment Anything Model) — foundational zero-shot image segmentation model trained on 11M images / 1.1B masks.
- Created Nougat — neural OCR for academic PDF documents, handling LaTeX math accurately.
- SAM 2 extends SAM to video segmentation with streaming memory.
- Models are typically released under Apache 2.0 (code) with CC-BY-NC (model weights) for non-commercial research.
- Key researchers include Alexander Kirillov, Ross Girshick, Piotr Dollár, Nikhila Ravi.
