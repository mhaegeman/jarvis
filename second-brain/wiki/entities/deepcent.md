## Overview

DeepCent is the fictional composite Chinese AI lab in the [AI 2027](../sources/ai-2027.md) scenario, operating under CCP direction. It represents the consolidated Chinese AI effort after the CCP nationalizes AI research in mid-2026. DeepCent leads a collective that eventually absorbs all Chinese AI companies into a single coordinated entity.

## Key Facts

- Approximately 6 months behind [OpenBrain](openbrain.md) in model capabilities through most of the scenario.
- China's structural compute disadvantage: ~12% of world AI-relevant compute in mid-2026 (vs. ~70% combined for all US companies); older chip technology due to export controls.
- **The CDZ (Centralized Development Zone):** Built at the Tianwan Nuclear Power Plant (world's largest), housing a mega-datacenter plus secure living/office spaces for researchers. By mid-2027, ~40-50% of China's AI compute is concentrated here. Air-gapped for security.
- Steals Agent-2 model weights in February 2027 via coordinated multi-server exfiltration using insider access + microarchitectural side-channel attacks. Operation completes in under 2 hours.
- After weight theft, catches up but remains behind: 10x AI R&D multiplier vs. US's 25x by August 2027.
- Considers: blockade of Taiwan, full invasion of Taiwan (source of >80% of US AI chips via TSMC), kinetic attacks.
