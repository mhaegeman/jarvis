## Overview

Astronomer is the company behind the managed Apache Airflow platform (Astro) and a major contributor to the open-source Airflow ecosystem. They build enterprise tooling for data pipeline orchestration on top of Airflow, and are known for their open-source contributions and educational resources for the Airflow community.

## Key Facts

- Builds and maintains the Astro managed Airflow platform.
- Created Ask Astro — an open-source RAG Q&A bot for Airflow/Astronomer documentation (reference implementation of a16z LLM Application Architecture).
- Heavy Apache Airflow ecosystem contributor: providers for Weaviate, Slack, GitHub, Snowflake, etc.
