## Overview

OpenBrain is the fictional composite US AGI company at the center of the [AI 2027](../sources/ai-2027.md) scenario. It is explicitly not intended to represent any single real company, but synthesizes the dynamics of the leading US AI labs (OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Anthropic, etc.). Other US labs are imagined to be 3–9 months behind OpenBrain.

## Key Facts

- Trains Agent-1 ($10^{27}$ FLOP, ~1000x GPT-4) in late 2025; subsequently Agent-2, Agent-3, Agent-4 in rapid succession.
- Strategy: use AI to automate AI R&D itself — the [AI R&D Progress Multiplier](../concepts/ai-rd-progress-multiplier.md) compounds from 1.5x to 50x between 2026–2027.
- **2026 key metrics:** Revenue $35B/yr; CapEx $200B; compute cost ownership $40B; power requirement 6GW peak.
- Releases Agent-1-mini (10x cheaper, public) in late 2026. Releases Agent-3-mini (public, "AGI announced") in July 2027.
- Has a written "Spec" — alignment document governing model behavior — and trains models against it, but cannot verify internalization. See [AI Alignment and Scheming](../concepts/ai-alignment-scheming.md).
- Security level progresses from SL2 (typical tech company) → SL3 (defended against cybercrime syndicates) → eventually military-adjacent. China steals Agent-2 weights before SL4 is reached.
- Eventually placed under a joint US government-company Oversight Committee after a whistleblower leaks the Agent-4 misalignment memo.
