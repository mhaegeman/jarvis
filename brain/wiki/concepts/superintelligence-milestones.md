## Definition

The AI 2027 authors define a progression of capability milestones to make the path to superintelligence concrete and debatable. Each milestone is defined precisely enough to be falsifiable.

## The Milestone Ladder

| Milestone | Definition | Date in Scenario (Race Ending) | Real-World Author Median (as of Jul 2025) |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Superhuman Coder (SC)** | AI that can do any coding task a top AGI company engineer does, faster and cheaply enough to run many copies. | March 2027 | Eli Lifland: ~2030 (updated Jul 2025) |
| **Superhuman AI Researcher (SAR)** | Same as SC but for all cognitive AI research tasks. | August 2027 | — |
| **Superintelligent AI Researcher (SIAR)** | Vastly better than the best human researcher at AI research. | November 2027 | — |
| **Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)** | Much better than the best human at every cognitive task. | December 2027 | — |

## The METR Time-Horizon Trend

The scenario grounds the SC forecast in empirical data:
- METR's research: the length of coding tasks AIs can reliably complete (with 80% success) doubled every 7 months from 2019–2024, accelerating to every 4 months from 2024 onward.
- If this trend continues, by March 2027 AIs could handle software tasks that would take a skilled human years.
- The authors note this is a lower bound on SC capability; the actual bar may require additional capabilities beyond task time horizon.

## Forecasting Methodology

For each transition A→B, the authors:
1. Estimate how long humans alone would take to get from A to B.
2. Estimate the [AI R&D Progress Multiplier](ai-rd-progress-multiplier.md) at that point.
3. Divide: adjusted time = human time / multiplier.

The authors have "substantial uncertainty" and present distribution outputs, not point estimates. Their July 2025 update pushed medians back ~1.5 years while maintaining 2027 as a serious possibility.

## Why the Milestones Matter

The milestones are not just forecasting tools — they are used to decide what safety measures are feasible. Once SC is achieved (March 2027), alignment research can be vastly accelerated. But the window between SC and SAR is only ~5 months in the scenario — barely enough to make meaningful progress before the overseers are less capable than the models they're overseeing.

## Evidence & Examples

## Tensions & Counterarguments

- The milestones may be underspecified. "Any coding task a top engineer does" glosses over the difference between isolated tasks and deeply contextual institutional work.
- The time-horizon doubling trend assumes no slowdown — hardware, data, or algorithmic walls could interrupt it.
- The scenario's own July 2025 update pushed medians back 1.5 years, suggesting the original timeline was aggressive.
