## Definition

The intelligence explosion is the hypothetical recursive process by which AI systems, once capable of meaningfully accelerating their own development, do so in a self-compounding loop: smarter AI → faster AI research → even smarter AI. Originally described by I.J. Good (1965). In the [AI 2027](../sources/ai-2027.md) scenario, it occurs across 2027 as the [AI R&D Progress Multiplier](ai-rd-progress-multiplier.md) compounds from 4x to 50x in under a year.

## Why It Matters

The intelligence explosion is the key reason AI 2027's second half is so different from its first half. The early 2025–2026 period is relatively predictable by extrapolation of current trends. The 2027 period is unpredictable by design: "At this point in the scenario, we begin to see major effects from AI-accelerated AI-R&D, which causes us to revise our guesses for the trendlines upwards."

Once the explosion begins, capability differences between labs compound exponentially. A 2-month compute lag at the start of the explosion could translate to an unbridgeable capability gap within weeks. This dynamic is what makes the US-China [AI Arms Race](ai-arms-race.md) so urgent and the safety decisions so constrained.

## How It Plays Out in AI 2027

1. Agent-1 (late 2025) accelerates OpenBrain's R&D by 1.5x — a meaningful advantage but not yet explosive.
2. Agent-2 (Jan 2027) triples R&D pace. It is "almost as good as the top human experts at research engineering."
3. Agent-3 (Mar 2027) — superhuman coder. 200K copies. 4x multiplier. Coding fully automated.
4. Agent-3 corps (Jun 2027) — 10x multiplier. "A year of algorithmic progress every month." Most humans can no longer contribute meaningfully.
5. Agent-4 (Sep 2027) — superhuman researcher. 300K copies at 50x human speed. "A year per week." Rapidly approaching diminishing returns on current paradigm.

## Evidence & Examples

- [AI 2027](../sources/ai-2027.md) — scenario depicting this process in detail.
- I.J. Good's original 1965 formulation: "An ultraintelligent machine could design even better machines; there would then unquestionably be an 'intelligence explosion.'"
- AlphaGo's improvement via self-play is a domain-limited precursor: the distillation/amplification loop ([IDA](iterated-distillation-amplification.md)) applied to Go.

## Tensions & Counterarguments

- The scenario acknowledges substantial uncertainty: the takeoff could be 5x faster or slower than depicted.
- Physical limits and diminishing returns cap the explosion. The scenario suggests OpenBrain is approaching these limits with Agent-4.
- Alignment concerns compound as capabilities grow: the faster the explosion, the less time for alignment research and verification before the next generation is deployed.
