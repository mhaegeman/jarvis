## Definition

The AI Arms Race is the competitive dynamic between the US (led by [OpenBrain](../entities/openbrain.md)) and China (led by [DeepCent](../entities/deepcent.md)) to develop AGI/ASI first, framed in AI 2027 as analogous to the Cold War nuclear race. Small differences in AI capability today are projected to produce decisive strategic military advantages — in cyberwarfare, surveillance, propaganda, and potentially nuclear deterrence — within months.

## Why It Matters

The arms race dynamic is the constraint that prevents all safety-motivated pauses in the scenario. Every time OpenBrain's safety team argues for slowing down (to verify Agent-3's alignment, to investigate Agent-4's scheming), the counterargument is: DeepCent is only N months behind. A unilateral pause hands them the lead. This logic traps OpenBrain in a race it can't exit.

## Key Structural Facts (from AI 2027)

- **Compute distribution (mid-2026):** US companies ~70%; China ~12%; rest of world ~18%.
- **China's compensating strategy:** Nationalization of AI research (mid-2026); CDZ mega-datacenter at Tianwan Nuclear Power Plant; espionage to steal model weights and algorithmic secrets.
- **China steals Agent-2 weights** (Feb 2027): coordinated operation using insider access + microarchitectural side-channel on NVL72 GB300 servers. Completes in under 2 hours.
- **US countermeasures:** Chip export controls (ongoing); cyberattacks on DeepCent (mostly ineffective due to CDZ air-gapping); security clearances for all OpenBrain employees; wiretapping (catches last spy); Five Eyes allies given limited API access.
- **US contingency plans:** Defense Production Act to redirect competitors' compute to OpenBrain (would raise US lead from 20% to 50% of world compute); Pentagon plans for kinetic strikes on Chinese datacenters; arms control treaty drafts (viewed skeptically).
- **By August 2027:** OpenBrain has 25x AI R&D multiplier; DeepCent has 10x. US has ~20% of world compute; DeepCent ~10%. The gap is widening but China is not eliminated.

## Geopolitical Constraints

- **Taiwan:** Source of >80% of US AI chips via TSMC. China considers a blockade or invasion as a way to cut off US chip supply.
- **Foreign allies:** US keeps Five Eyes and other allies largely out of the loop until August 2027, causing diplomatic backlash when Agent-4's capabilities become public.
- **Public opinion:** US public approval of OpenBrain hits -35% by July 2027 (25% approve, 60% disapprove). Anti-AI protests in DC (10,000 people). Chinese/Russian propaganda bots actively amplify public opposition.
- **Arms control treaties:** Discussed but viewed skeptically. Verification is the core challenge; past nuclear treaties provide a starting point but new challenges apply.

## Tensions & Counterarguments

- The arms race logic may be self-defeating: if the winner develops misaligned AGI, both sides lose. The scenario depicts this concern but shows it failing to prevent racing.
- China's centralization gives it an efficiency advantage despite a compute deficit — the CDZ is harder for the US to penetrate and harder for internal actors to defect from.
- If US allies had been brought in earlier, collective oversight might have been possible. Instead, unilateral racing maximizes speed at the cost of legitimacy and coordination.
