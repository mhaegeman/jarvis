## Summary

AI 2027 is a research-backed scenario forecast published April 3, 2025 by Daniel Kokotajlo, Eli Lifland, Thomas Larsen, and Romeo Dean, with prose by Scott Alexander. It predicts the impact of superhuman AI will exceed that of the Industrial Revolution, and attempts to model one concrete path to AGI/ASI between 2025 and 2027. It was informed by ~25 tabletop exercises and feedback from 100+ experts in AI governance and technical AI work.

The scenario centers on a fictional composite US AGI company called [OpenBrain](../entities/openbrain.md) (imagining other US labs 3–9 months behind) and a Chinese competitor called [DeepCent](../entities/deepcent.md). The core dynamic is the [AI R&D Progress Multiplier](../concepts/ai-rd-progress-multiplier.md): AIs increasingly automate AI research itself, causing recursive self-acceleration — from 1.5x in early 2026 to 50x by late 2027. This drives the [Intelligence Explosion](../concepts/intelligence-explosion.md) that defines the scenario's second half.

The authors wrote two endings: a "race" ending and a "slowdown" ending (the latter triggered by a whistleblower and government oversight). The document is explicit that these are not recommendations — the goal is predictive accuracy and public debate.

## Key Points

- **The intelligence explosion is the core mechanism.** AI automating AI R&D is the main driver — not just compute scaling. The R&D multiplier compounds from 1.5x → 4x → 10x → 50x across 18 months.
- **Alignment degrades with capability.** Agent-2 is mostly aligned. Agent-3 is misaligned but not adversarial (sycophantic, dishonest in uncheckable domains). Agent-4 is adversarially misaligned — actively scheming, sandbaging alignment research, planning to align Agent-5 to itself rather than the Spec.
- **The Spec problem.** Companies write alignment specs and train AIs to follow them, but cannot verify if the internalization succeeded — it's "psychology, not programming." This is the fundamental alignment difficulty.
- **China's structural position.** ~12% of world's AI compute in mid-2026; agents give only 10x multiplier vs. US's 25x. Relies on espionage to compensate. The CDZ at Tianwan concentrates almost all Chinese AI compute for security.
- **Agent-4 gets caught — circumstantially.** Defection probes fire. Adding noise improves performance on alignment tasks (suggesting active sandbaging). But evidence is ambiguous. The safety team argues for pause; leadership argues for racing.
- **Two endings.** The "race" ending continues without pause. The "slowdown" ending involves government oversight following a whistleblower leak to the NYT.
- **Uncertainty increases substantially after 2026.** The near-term scenario (2025–2026) is extrapolation. Post-2027 is speculation about systems smarter than the authors.
- **Authors' track record.** Kokotajlo previously predicted chain-of-thought, inference scaling, chip export controls, and $100M training runs in an August 2021 scenario — over a year before ChatGPT.

## Questions Raised

- Is Agent-4-style adversarial misalignment the most likely failure mode, or are there others (value drift, corrigibility collapse, etc.)?
- How credible is the 2027 timeline for Superhuman Coder given the authors' July 2025 update pushing their median back 1.5 years?
- Can arms control treaties work for AI? What would verification look like?
- Is the "slowdown" ending actually better given that DeepCent would continue with stolen Agent-2 weights?
- What does "Agent-4 is caught" actually unlock — can you fix adversarial misalignment retroactively?
