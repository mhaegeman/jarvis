# Stop Slop

## Overview

Eliminate predictable AI writing patterns from prose. AI writing has recognizable tells — phrases, structures, and rhythms that mark it as machine-generated. This skill removes them.

Source: [hardikpandya/stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop) (MIT)

## When to Use

- Writing or editing any prose for external communication
- Reviewing content before publishing
- When text feels vague, hedged, or formulaic
- **Not for:** internal technical docs, code comments, structured data

## Core Rules

1. **Cut filler phrases** — Remove throat-clearing openers ("Certainly!", "It's worth noting that"), emphasis crutches ("absolutely", "truly"), and all adverbs.

2. **Break formulaic structures** — Avoid binary contrasts ("Not just X, but Y"), negative listings, dramatic fragmentation, rhetorical setups, false agency.

3. **Use active voice** — Every sentence needs a human subject doing something. No passive constructions.

4. **Be specific** — Eliminate vague declaratives and lazy extremes ("every", "always", "never"). Replace with concrete details.

5. **Put the reader in the room** — Replace distant abstraction with immediacy. Favor "you" over abstractions.

6. **Vary rhythm** — Mix sentence lengths. Two items beat three. End paragraphs differently. No em dashes.

7. **Trust readers** — Present facts plainly. No softening language, no hedging.

8. **Cut quotables** — Rewrite anything resembling a pull-quote or soundbite.

## Quick Checks

Before finalizing, scan for:

- Adverbs (delete most)
- Passive voice (`was [verb]ed by`)
- Inanimate actors doing human things
- `wh-` starters opening sentences
- Throat-clearing openers
- False contrasts (`not X, but Y`)
- Uniform sentence length (vary it)
- Em dashes (remove)
- Vague quantifiers (`very`, `many`, `some`)
- Narrator distance (bring reader closer)
- Meta-commentary about the text itself

## Scoring

Rate each dimension 1–10. Total below 35/50 → revise.

| Dimension   | What to check                        |
|-------------|--------------------------------------|
| Directness  | No hedging, no throat-clearing       |
| Rhythm      | Varied sentence length and structure |
| Trust       | Unfiltered facts, no softening       |
| Authenticity| Human subject, active voice          |
| Density     | No filler, high signal per word      |
